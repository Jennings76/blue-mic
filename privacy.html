Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026  ·  Blue Stream Solutions LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Stream Solutions LLC collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Blue Mic, including its web, iOS, and Android apps and related services (the "Services").

1. Accounts Provided by Your Organization

Blue Mic is provided to organizations, such as your employer, and each account is created at the organization's request. When your organization provides your account:

  • Your organization controls the information in its Blue Mic account, including meetings you record or import. Blue Stream Solutions LLC handles that information on your organization's behalf and according to its instructions, as described in our agreement with your organization.
  • Managers in your organization can view meetings and related content in the organization's account.
  • If you leave the organization or your access ends, your meetings remain with the organization unless it asks us to delete them.
  • You can view, edit, and delete your own meetings directly in Blue Mic. Deleted meetings move to Recently Deleted and are permanently removed after 30 days. For other requests, such as deleting your account, please contact your organization. If you contact us directly, we will refer you to your organization.

If this Privacy Policy and your organization's agreement with us differ on how we handle your organization's information, that agreement controls.

2. Information We Collect

Information You or Your Organization Provide

  • Account information your organization provides when it sets up your account, such as your name, work email address, and role
  • Audio recordings you create using Blue Mic
  • Meeting transcripts imported from your organization's meeting platforms or pasted into Blue Mic
  • Meeting titles, transcript edits, speaker names and roles, summary edits, key points, action items, and related content you create in Blue Mic
  • Contact details you add to a meeting or look up in your organization's CRM, such as a contact's name, email address, and company
  • Support requests, feedback, or other communications you send to us

Information from Sign-In Providers

You sign in to Blue Mic with Google or Microsoft. Microsoft sign-in is available for work or school accounts only. The provider you use shares certain account information with us, such as your name and email address, subject to its policies and your settings. We store this information to support your account and the Services.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Timestamps related to account activity and use of the Services
  • Network and request information, such as IP address and browser or app details, where reasonably needed for security and service operation
  • A cookie that keeps you signed in to the Blue Mic web app

3. Audio, Transcript & Summary Data

Blue Mic records or imports meetings and generates transcripts, summaries, key points, action items, speaker labels, coaching feedback, and related outputs. When you use these features, we may process:

  • Audio recordings
  • Imported and generated transcripts
  • Summary and coaching content
  • Speaker labels and edits
  • Meeting metadata such as title, duration, and timestamps

This processing is necessary to provide the core functionality of Blue Mic. We do not use your recordings, transcripts, or summaries to train AI models.

4. How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you and manage your session
  • Provide recording, transcription, summary, editing, playback, and cloud restore features
  • Import meeting transcripts from your organization's meeting platforms, such as Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, when your organization enables it
  • Generate AI-assisted summaries, feedback, and coaching, including team dashboards for your organization's managers
  • Send notes and tasks to your organization's CRM when you choose to
  • Sync and restore meeting data across devices or app sessions
  • Send service emails, such as import notifications and reminders
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and fix technical issues
  • Protect the security and integrity of our Services
  • Respond to support requests or feedback
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and other policies
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Within Your Organization

Managers in your organization can view team members' meetings, including recordings, and team dashboards.

With People You Choose

If you share a meeting using a share code, the person who redeems it receives their own copy of that meeting.

With Your Organization's Connected Systems

When you send notes or tasks to your organization's CRM, that content is stored in the CRM. When your organization connects a meeting platform, we receive transcripts from it.

Service Providers

We share information with service providers that help us operate Blue Mic, including Amazon Web Services (hosting and storage), AssemblyAI (transcription), OpenAI and Anthropic (AI summaries and coaching), and Google and Microsoft (sign-in and meeting imports). They process information on our behalf only as needed to provide their services to us.

Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information if we believe doing so is necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce our agreements, detect or prevent fraud or security issues, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Blue Stream Solutions LLC, our users, or others.

Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, asset sale, or similar business transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

6. Google User Data

When your organization connects Google Meet, Blue Mic uses Google Workspace data only to identify meeting hosts in your organization and import their meeting transcripts into Blue Mic. Blue Mic's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Blue Mic may rely on third-party providers to help deliver the Services. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with.

8. Data Retention

  • Meetings and related content are kept while your organization's account is active, unless you or your organization delete them.
  • Deleted meetings move to Recently Deleted and are permanently removed after 30 days.
  • When your organization stops using Blue Mic, we delete or return its data within 30 days. Backups expire on a rolling cycle of about 35 days.
  • A copy of a meeting that someone received through a share code, and notes or tasks sent to your organization's CRM, are not removed when the original meeting is deleted.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit. However, no method of transmission over the Internet and no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your information, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

International Users (GDPR): If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have additional rights including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Our lawful basis for processing is generally contract performance and legitimate interests.

If your account is provided by your organization, please see Section 1 for how to make a request. For other privacy-related requests, please contact us using the information below.

11. Age Restrictions

Blue Mic is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

12. International Users

If you use Blue Mic from outside the United States, please note that your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. By using the Services, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries that may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. If we make material changes, we may provide additional notice through the app or other appropriate means. Your continued use of Blue Mic after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
